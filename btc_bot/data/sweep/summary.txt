How to read this: all variants trade the same candles over the same period. R is one unit of risk, so totals compare fairly across windows. The cost line is an assumption, not your broker's real spread. One year is one market regime; choosing the best of several variants on the same year tends to look better than it will trade live, so treat a winner as a candidate to forward-test on the demo, not as a result. A window that wins only by trading more often is the first one a real cost will hurt.
